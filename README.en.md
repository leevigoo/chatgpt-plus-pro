## 1. Background

OpenAI's **ChatGPT Plus** ($20/mo) is the most widely used AI subscription today, but users in mainland China typically hit three blockers when upgrading:

1. **Payment restrictions** — OpenAI only accepts Visa / MasterCard issued outside restricted regions, and validates the billing address.
2. **Virtual cards under heavy risk control** — Depay / OneKey Card / WildCard and similar services are increasingly throttled, with **declining success rates**.
3. **Shared / carpool accounts are unstable** — they can be kicked anytime due to refund disputes or login conflicts, and your personal data ends up mixed with strangers'.

In 2026 there are basically two methods that still reliably work: **own a real overseas credit card**, or **use a third-party top-up service that upgrades your own account directly**.
